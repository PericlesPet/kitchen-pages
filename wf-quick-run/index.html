No-cook combos

  • Turkey or chicken wrap: Carb Bueno or Egglife wrap + 4 slices turkey / pulled rotisserie breast + 2 tbsp Greek yogurt mixed with mustard. ~30–35 g P.
  • Yogurt cup: Chobani 20 g or siggi's straight from the fridge. 14–20 g P.
  • Shake + scoop: Chobani 30 g drink, or milk blended/shaken with a scoop of your protein powder.
  • Shrimp cocktail: thawed cooked shrimp (fridge overnight or cold water 10 min) + cocktail sauce + lemon. ~18 g P per 3 oz.
  • Snack plate: Chomps stick + cottage cheese + egg white bites (eat cold or 30 s in the microwave).

Not on the Whole Foods Amazon storefront (so not listed): Oikos Pro / Triple Zero, Fairlife milk, Core Power, Good Culture plain 16 oz, Fage 0%, Ratio, Quest. Checked but under 10 g P/100 kcal: string cheese (8.8), hard-cooked eggs (8.3), edamame (7.9), Organic Valley Protein Plus whole milk, protein bars.
